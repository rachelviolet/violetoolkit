Attackers use Microsoft Teams to target victims

Did you see it? Attackers are now using Microsoft Teams to spread Ransomware (a virus that steals your data). Spam emails and malicious users identifying themselves as a “Help Desk Manager” are terribly common nowadays.

“How do I protect myself from cybersecurity threats?”

The answer is surprisingly simple: 1) Understand what and who you’re interacting with. 2) Use a strong password. 3) Use 2FA (Two-Factor Authentication). 4) Do not share your login credentials with anyone. 5) Do not download any files you weren't expecting, regardless of how innocent they seem to be. 6) Understand how modern cybernetic threats work.
