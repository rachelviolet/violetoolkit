What Are Hardware Requirements?

Think of your computer as a house. For home appliances to properly work, they need enough space and to be compatible with the outlets that your house have. If you plug in a 220V appliance into a 120V outlet, you might run into serious issues. And for certain applications to properly work, your computer must have enough storage and to be compatible with the app. Usually, the application won't run if it isn't compatible. Forcing it to run could or could not lead to unexpected events, like data corruption.
