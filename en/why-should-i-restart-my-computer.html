Why Should I Restart My Computer?

Think of your computer as a house. Things like cooking and using the bathroom make the house dirtier. Sooner or later, cleaning the house will be necessary. To computers — laptops, phones and similar devices — the applications may not work properly once the machine is up for too long. If that happens, restart the application first. If that doesn't fix it, restart the computer.
