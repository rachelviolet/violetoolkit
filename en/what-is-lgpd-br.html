(Brazilian law) What is the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados Pessoais (LGPD)?

Original URL of the law (in Brazilian Portuguese): L13709/2018

The General Law of Protection of Personal Data, LGPD in Portuguese, is a Brazilian law that seeks to protect personal information from collection, use and sharing, that were made inappropriately.

Some of the information in the LGPD include: How the data can be used, how the data can be stored, for how long and in which situations.

Beside the LGPD in Brazil, we have other data protection laws in other regions, such as the GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) in the European Union (EU), the CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act), in California, United States (USA) and the POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act), in South Africa.
