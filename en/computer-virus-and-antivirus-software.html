Computer Viruses and Antivirus Software

Think of your computer as a house. It can be a safe place, but not 100% safe. Someone can spy on you through the window, someone can sneak in if you don't close the door(s) and windows, someone can steal your money without you noticing. An antivirus software is like a security system for your house. It may notice that an app is trying to access your files and tamper with important settings, they may remember that an app was already discovered to be a virus a few days ago, so it’ll block the virus, allowing you to let the program run regardless. Antiviruses aren't perfect and your attention is the best way to prevent digital attacks!
