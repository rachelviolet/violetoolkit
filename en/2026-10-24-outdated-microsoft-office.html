Statistics: Outdated Microsoft Office

2026-10-24

According to StatCounter, nearly 30% of computers still use an outdated version of Microsoft Windows. According to Intra2net, 82% of small businesses still use an outdated version of Microsoft Office. Even as far back as 2017, a survey from DART tech showed that most businesses were using outdated versions as well. This raises an important question: Why do we prioritize updating Windows but overlook the critical need to update Microsoft Office?
