How you can defend yourself from these cybersecurity threats

2026-10-20

Cybercriminals use multiple AI tools (like deepfake phishing and automated hacking) to attack individuals and all types of business, from small companies to international enterprises. Here’s how you can defend yourself from these cybersecurity threats: 1. Avoid suspicious apps and services: Choose known developers. 2. Think before clicking/interacting: Phishing and social engineering attacks are getting more sophisticated. 3. Verify permissions from existing apps: Does that app that doesn't need to see your face really need access to your camera? 4. Use multi-factor authentication: Passwords aren't enough. 5. Use access control: An employee must only be able to access what they need to do their job. 6. Use a good antivirus tool: You can defend yourself from AI attacks with AI defense. 7. Monitor and audit your network/website: Common tools include Darktrace, Splunk and CrowdStrike.
