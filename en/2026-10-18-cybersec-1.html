I wanted to share interesting cybersecurity concepts that I learned today! TPM (Trusted Platform Module): Microcontroller that stores PCRs. PCR (Platform Configuration Registers): Stores hashes that determines hardware integrity. RIM (Reference Integrity Manifest): Contains hashes that compare PCR hash values with its own to verify if the hardware was tampered with or not. I might share what I've been learning more often.
