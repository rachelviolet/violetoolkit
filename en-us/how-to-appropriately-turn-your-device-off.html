How To Appropriately Turn Your Device Off

Do not forcefully turn your device off, unless the device has crashed. To turn a Computer/Laptop/MacBook/iMac off: Click on whichever power menu is available (Start Menu on Windows and many Linux distributions) and click "Power Off" or "Shutdown". To turn an Android phone off: Press the Power/Side Button and press "Power Off". To turn an iPhone OFF: Press and hold the Side Button and; If you have an iPhone with the home button, drag the slider. If you have an iphone without the home button, press and hold the Volume Button as well until the the sliders appear, then drag the Power Off slider. To turn a PlayStation 5 off: Select Power from the control center, then select "Turn Off PS5". To turn an XBOX One off: Press the Xbox button on your controller, then choose "Turn off console". To turn a Nintendo Switch off: Press and hold the power button for 3 seconds.
