How To Never Get Scammed, Hacked or Infected With A Computer Virus?

1) Use an antivirus software. 2) Do not open or download suspicious links, emails, files or apps. 3) Keep your apps and your operating system updated. 4) Do not share your personal information with untrustworthy sources. 5) Use a strong password. 6) Use 2FA (Two-Factor Authentication). 7) Continue learning about online threats. 8) Use trustworthy websites. Browse safely!
